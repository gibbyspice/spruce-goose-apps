ChessRealm: Openings

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

ChessRealm: Openings is an app for learning chess openings. It is published by Timothy Gibbons ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases.
  • The only thing the app sends over the internet is a chess position, when you ask for an engine evaluation.

Information that stays on your device

The app saves these on your device so it can remember your progress:

  • your study sessions, mastery records and saved sessions;
  • trial and certificate results;
  • your settings, including whether reminders are on.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time in the app under Settings, then Reset all progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Your device's own iCloud or computer backups may include it, under Apple's terms.

Engine evaluation

When the app shows an engine evaluation, it sends the current board position to the Lichess cloud evaluation service to get it. Only the chess position is sent, with no identifier, account or cookie, though like any internet request it carries basic technical information such as your IP address. Lichess's privacy policy is at lichess.org/privacy.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders and allow notifications, the app shows study reminders. They are scheduled on your device. The app never registers your device with a notification server, and we receive nothing when a reminder appears. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Sharing

When you earn a certificate you can share a short message about it with the share button. The app hands the message to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you tap share, and what happens next is up to the app you pick.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising and the app has no in-app purchases.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent information to anyone.
  • We do not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Apart from engine evaluation, the app does not connect to the internet. Its content is built in.

Children

The app does not require or collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, for example by adding purchases, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
