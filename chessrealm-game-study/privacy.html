ChessRealm: Game Study

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

ChessRealm: Game Study is an app for studying famous chess games. It is published by Timothy Gibbons ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • If you buy Full access, Apple and our purchase service handle the purchase. We do not see your payment details.
  • Apart from purchases, the only thing the app sends over the internet is a chess position, when you ask for an engine evaluation.

Information that stays on your device

The app saves these on your device so it can remember your progress:

  • study sessions you complete (which game, your accuracy, the date and how long it took);
  • missed key moments queued for review, with their review schedule;
  • seals, exam results, study XP and streak days;
  • starred games and any game you left part-way through;
  • your settings, including study interests, daily goal and whether reminders are on.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time in the app under Settings, then Reset study progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Your device's own iCloud or computer backups may include it, under Apple's terms.

Engine evaluation

Engine evaluation is optional. When you turn it on in a game, the app sends the current board position to the Lichess cloud evaluation service. If the service is unavailable, the app evaluates the position on your device instead. Only the chess position is sent, with no identifier, account or cookie, though like any internet request it carries basic technical information such as your IP address. Lichess's privacy policy is at lichess.org/privacy.

Purchases

The app is free to use, and Full access is an optional one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, card number or Apple Account details.

To confirm that a purchase belongs to you and to restore it on a new device, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for the app, the record of your purchase from Apple, and basic technical information that comes with any internet request, such as device model, operating system version, country and IP address. This information is not used for advertising and is not sold. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Apple's is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

To ask about the purchase record held for the app, or to ask for it to be deleted, email us at the address below.

Reminders

Review reminders are off unless you turn them on in Settings and allow notifications. The app then schedules at most one reminder for your next due review. They are scheduled on your device. The app never registers your device with a notification server, and we receive nothing when a reminder appears. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Sharing

You can share a seal you earn. The app makes an image and a short message on your device and hands them to the share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap share, and what happens next is up to the app you pick.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent information to anyone.
  • We do not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Apart from engine evaluation and purchases, the app does not connect to the internet. Its content is built in.

Children

The app does not require any personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases should be made by a parent or guardian, or with their permission. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
