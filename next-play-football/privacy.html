Next Play Football

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Next Play Football: Xs & Os is an app that teaches football formations, coverages, reads and situations. It is published by Timothy Gibbons ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your name, positions and progress stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • If you buy the full version, Apple and our purchase service handle the purchase. We do not see your payment details.

Information that stays on your device

The app saves these on your device so it can remember where you left off:

  • the first name or nickname you type in, if you choose to enter one;
  • the positions and level you pick;
  • your answers, points, streaks and settings.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it. You can also erase it at any time in the app under Settings, then Reset Progress.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, card number or Apple Account details.

To confirm that a purchase belongs to you and to restore it on a new device, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for the app, the record of your purchase from Apple, and basic technical information that comes with any internet request, such as device model, operating system version, country and IP address. This information is not used for advertising and is not sold. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Apple's is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent information to anyone.
  • We do not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or notifications.
  • Apart from purchases, the app does not connect to the internet. Every play, drawing and explanation it shows is built in.

Children

Next Play Football is designed to be suitable for children, including children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to play, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A child may type a first name or nickname, which stays on the device.

Purchases are made through the App Store and should be made by a parent or guardian, or with their permission.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, or believe the app holds information about your child that you want removed, contact us at the address below and we will help.

Your choices

  • Erase everything stored on your device: Settings, then Reset Progress, or delete the app.
  • Ask about or request deletion of the purchase record held for the app: email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
