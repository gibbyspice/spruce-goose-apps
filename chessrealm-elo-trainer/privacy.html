ChessRealm: ELO Trainer

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

ChessRealm: ELO Trainer is a chess tactics training app. It is published by Timothy Gibbons ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your training, ratings and settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • If you buy Premium, Apple and our purchase service handle the purchase. We do not see your payment details.

Information that stays on your device

The app saves these on your device so it can remember your progress:

  • your training sets, puzzle attempts, cycles and solving times;
  • your Elo test results, goals and streaks;
  • games you choose to import by pasting them in;
  • your settings.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time in the app under Settings, then Reset progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Engine analysis

When the app shows engine analysis after a puzzle, it sends the board position to the Lichess cloud evaluation service to get the evaluation. Only the chess position is sent, nothing about you, though like any internet request it carries basic technical information such as your IP address. Lichess's privacy policy is at lichess.org/privacy.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, card number or Apple Account details.

To confirm that a purchase belongs to you and to restore it on a new device, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for the app, the record of your purchase from Apple, and basic technical information that comes with any internet request, such as device model, operating system version, country and IP address. This information is not used for advertising and is not sold. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Apple's is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules them on your device. We do not operate a notification server and receive nothing when a reminder appears. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent information to anyone.
  • We do not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Apart from engine analysis and purchases, the app does not connect to the internet. The puzzles are built in.

Children

The app does not require any personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases should be made by a parent or guardian, or with their permission. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, contact us at the address below.

Your choices

  • Erase everything stored on your device: Settings, then Reset progress, or delete the app.
  • Ask about or request deletion of the purchase record held for the app: email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
